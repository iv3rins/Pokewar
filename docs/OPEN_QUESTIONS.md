# Open Questions

Do not implement unresolved items by assumption. Move each answered item into the normative rules or an ADR and record the decision date.

## OQ-002: Normal-Mode Replacement Choice Privacy

Reserve characters are hidden. Confirm whether the replacement-selection phase should reveal the selected character only after confirmation, and whether selection is simultaneous with any other pending transition. Current architecture assumes only the dying player selects while the room is paused.

## OQ-003: Match Summary Fields

Full replay retention is fixed at 14 days. The exact long-term anonymized summary fields are not yet decided. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal or chat data; chat is deferred.

## OQ-005: Existing Account Avatar Enrollment

The avatar system will provide 32 reviewed local court-emblem avatars. Guests receive a random immutable avatar and new accounts choose once during registration. Decide how accounts created before avatar rollout choose their one-time avatar after the final asset IDs and artwork are supplied.
